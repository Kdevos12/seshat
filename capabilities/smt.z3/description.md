Runs z3 on an SMT-LIB 2 script and returns sat, unsat, unknown or timeout from the first line of its output, with the unsat core when the script names its assertions. The harness translates a Lean goal into such a script (arithmetic over integers and reals, uninterpreted functions and sorts) and asserts the negation of the goal, so `unsat` means the goal follows from the hypotheses and premises. That is evidence, not a proof: the claim stays open (at most a `computed` edge) until the premises of the core are handed to `grind`, `aesop` or `hammer` and Lean accepts the result through validate. z3 is a process: the capability is unavailable where it is not installed.
