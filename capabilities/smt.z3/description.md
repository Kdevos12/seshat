Runs z3 on an SMT-LIB 2 script and returns sat, unsat, unknown or timeout from the first line of its output, with the unsat core when the script names its assertions. The harness translates a Lean goal into such a script (arithmetic over integers and reals, uninterpreted functions and sorts) and asserts the negation of the goal, so `unsat` means the goal follows from the hypotheses and premises. That is evidence, not a proof: the claim stays open (at most a `computed` edge) until the premises of the core are handed to `grind`, `aesop` or `hammer` and Lean accepts the result through validate. z3 is a process: the capability is unavailable where it is not installed. Two recipes of `@seshat/classical` (`smt-recipes.ts`, plan change CX7) use z3's other engines through the same input: Spacer on Horn clauses (`(set-logic HORN)`), for the inductive invariant of a loop or process given as transitions, and Optimize (`maximize`, `minimize`, `assert-soft`, `(get-objectives)`), for a small optimum; the screen admits these four commands, which read and write no file. Their answers are read strictly, and an invariant or an optimum becomes an `open` claim with an `smt_recipe` evidence node, never a status.
