Searches for Lean declarations that fit a query: the missing name of an `unknown identifier`, or the goal of a hole. It is called by the harness after a check, never by the model, and answers names: `hits` (each with the backend that found it), the backends that answered (`used`) and those that were left out or failed, with why (`skipped`). Three backends, each optional: `remote` (an HTTP search service for natural-language and goal queries), `local` (a local index of declarations ranked by BM25, no model) and `pattern` (a Loogle-like service by address, or a match of the goal's conclusion over the local index when no address is set). A hit is a name that the backend says exists: the harness checks it with Lean in the run's environment before anything is shown, and shows Lean's signature, not the backend's text. With `private` set, no backend that sends the query to a third party is asked.
