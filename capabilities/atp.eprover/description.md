Runs E (the eprover binary the host has installed, never bundled) on a TPTP problem and returns its SZS status and the axioms its proof uses. The harness translates a Lean goal (arithmetic, uninterpreted functions and sorts) to TFF with the hypotheses and premises as axioms and the goal as the conjecture. `theorem` is evidence, not a proof: the status is read from the SZS line and the exit code, and the claim stays open (at most a `computed` edge) until the used premises are handed to `grind`, `aesop` or `hammer` and Lean accepts the result through validate. E is a process, GPL-2.0 software that is only ever invoked, never linked: the capability is unavailable where it is not installed.
