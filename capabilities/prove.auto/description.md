Tries Lean's classical tactics on a goal you state and tells you which one closes it. The goal is a statement in signature form. A router reads it (linear arithmetic over the naturals or integers goes to omega, linear real arithmetic to linarith, polynomial identities to ring, finite domains to decide, first-order goals to aesop and grind) and the tactics the host's Lean has run in parallel, a few seconds each, the first success stopping the others. You get the tactic and the text to put after `by`: put it in your own proof and check it with lean_check; this tool proves nothing for the harness. The harness already ran this on the problem's target and its subgoals before any model, and the briefing says what failed there: call it for a lemma or a step you introduce. `polyrith`, `canonical` and the hammer are tried only if the host's Lean has them.
