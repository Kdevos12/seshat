# Code of conduct

Seshat follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be respectful and welcoming, criticize ideas and not people, and assume good faith. Harassment and
personal attacks are not accepted in issues, pull requests or any other project space.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer, Kyllian de Vos ([@Kdevos12](https://github.com/Kdevos12)),
privately. Reports are handled in confidence. The maintainer may remove content, or block a participant,
that breaks this code.
